# Reports & Documentation

This folder contains executive summaries, supporting documentation, and reporting materials related to the Operational Performance Dashboard project.

The documentation explains the business problem, dashboard functionality, KPI tracking methodology, and operational reporting objectives supported by the system.

## Documentation Topics

- Executive summary
- Business problem analysis
- Workforce analytics overview
- KPI reporting structure
- Dashboard functionality
- Operational reporting workflows
- Business impact and reporting improvements

The reports provide business context and explain how the dashboard supported operational decision-making and workforce performance monitoring.
